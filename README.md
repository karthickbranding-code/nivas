# Skills & Prompts

Reusable, version-controlled AI workflows for industrial research, B2B content, and thought leadership.

## Skills

| Skill | Purpose | File |
|---|---|---|
| White Paper | Research, plan, draft, fact-check, design, and repurpose an evidence-led white paper | [Open skill](skills/whitepaper/SKILL.md) |

| B2B Lead Generation Campaign | Connect audience research, offers, landing pages, follow-up, and pipeline metrics | [Open skill](skills/b2b-lead-generation-campaign/SKILL.md) |

## How to use
1. Open a skill's SKILL.md.
2. Provide topic, audience, business objective, approved facts, and source material.
3. Follow the stages rather than jumping straight to the final deliverable.
4. Verify sources and claims; obtain technical, legal, security, or customer approval where needed.
5. Update the skill as you learn and commit changes with a clear message.

## Principles
- Do not invent citations, statistics, customer results, product capabilities, or quotations.
- Separate verified evidence from assumptions and hypotheses.
- Keep product and performance claims within approved evidence.
- Measure business outcomes, not only content volume or vanity metrics.

## Suggested workflow
Industrial Pain-Point Research → White Paper → Industrial AI Case Study / LinkedIn Content → Lead Generation Campaign → Analytics and learning.
