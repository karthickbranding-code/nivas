# B2B Industrial Lead Generation Campaign Skill

## Goal
Build a coherent path from an industrial buyer's problem to a useful next step and qualified business conversation. Leads are not impressions, clicks, or unqualified form fills.

## Inputs
Offer and deliverables; target industries, roles, company profile, and geography; problem hypothesis and evidence; approved positioning; landing page and analytics; follow-up capacity; budget, timeline, privacy requirements, and success criteria.

## Campaign workflow
1. Define ideal customer profile: company type, industry, geography, operating context, buyer role, influencers, and exclusions.
2. Identify a verified trigger or pain that creates urgency.
3. Describe a recognisable workflow and consequence.
4. Create a useful offer: assessment, checklist, benchmark conversation, workshop, or demo. State scope, inputs, turnaround, eligibility, and limitations.
5. Build the message hierarchy: problem → insight → approach → proof → offer → CTA.
6. Create a landing page: audience-specific headline, problem, value, deliverables, process, evidence, limitations, form, privacy notice, and confirmation.
7. Plan distribution through organic LinkedIn, executive thought leadership, relevant communities/events, partners, and paid channels only when justified.
8. Define follow-up owner, response time, qualification questions, useful next action, and CRM fields.
9. Measure reach → relevant engagement → visits → CTA clicks → valid submissions → qualified leads → meetings → opportunities → revenue where attribution supports it.
10. Optimise one variable at a time: audience, offer, hook, landing page, form friction, or follow-up.

## Offer prompt
> Design three credible lead-generation offers for [industrial audience] facing [problem]. Approved capability: [facts]. For each, specify reader value, exact deliverables, required inputs, fulfilment effort, qualification criteria, landing-page CTA, limitations, and success metric. Do not promise unapproved outcomes or turnaround times.

## Landing-page prompt
> Draft a B2B landing page for [offer] aimed at [buyer role/industry]. Include headline, problem, value, deliverables, process, evidence, fit, form fields, privacy reassurance, FAQ, and CTA. Use only approved claims. State what is and is not included. Do not invent logos, metrics, certifications, or guarantees.

## Qualification
Capture only information needed for relevant follow-up: business email/name, company/role, industry/site context, problem area, desired outcome/timing, and contact preference/consent where applicable. Follow relevant privacy rules. Do not imply a form submission guarantees an assessment or result.

## KPI definitions
- Landing-page conversion rate = valid submissions ÷ landing-page sessions, using a consistent analytics definition.
- Lead qualification rate = qualified leads ÷ valid leads.
- Meeting rate = meetings held ÷ qualified leads.
- Opportunity rate = opportunities created ÷ qualified leads, with attribution window stated.
- Cost per qualified lead = campaign cost ÷ qualified leads.
Pipeline and revenue attribution require an agreed model and window.

## Review cadence
Weekly: delivery, traffic quality, CTA activity, form issues, response SLA, lead quality.
Monthly: qualified leads, meetings, opportunities, cost efficiency, offer fit, and lessons.
Do not optimise for cheap leads when sales relevance is poor.

## Rules
Never fabricate proof or guarantee ROI. Ensure the team can fulfil the offer before promoting it. Align content, landing page, form, and follow-up. Distinguish an assessment from a deployment. Treat analytics as evidence, not proof of causality without a valid design.
