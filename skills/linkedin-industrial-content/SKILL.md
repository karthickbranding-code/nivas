# Industrial LinkedIn Content Skill

## Objective
Build trust and qualified conversations with industrial decision-makers. Do not optimise only for impressions or publish repetitive product posters. Each post needs a defined audience, purpose, evidence, format, and next action.

## Content funnel
1. Awareness: verified industry insight and operating realities.
2. Problem: root causes, consequences, risks, and workarounds.
3. Education: technology, data readiness, implementation, trade-offs, and limitations.
4. Use case / proof: approved case studies, demonstrations, architectures, and measured results.
5. Conversation: diagnostic questions and practitioner perspectives.
6. Trust: methods, governance, security, human oversight, and lessons.
7. Lead: useful checklist, assessment, framework, or technical discussion.
8. Conversion: clear consultation/demo/assessment offer with accurate expectations.
9. Retention: implementation insights, FAQs, and customer enablement.

## Planning process
1. Choose one target role and one real problem.
2. Select funnel stage and reader action.
3. Verify evidence and approved product facts.
4. Choose format: text insight, diagram, carousel, video, case study, document, poll, or executive viewpoint.
5. Write one hook, useful body, takeaway, and relevant CTA.
6. Check claims, legibility, accessibility, and brand consistency.
7. Log URL, date, format, audience, and hypothesis after publishing.
8. Review results after a consistent measurement window.
9. Record learning and plan follow-up based on evidence.

## Post brief
- Target role
- Industry/process
- Pain point
- Funnel stage and goal
- Reader insight and evidence/source
- Approved product connection
- Format, hook, argument, CTA
- Success metric and follow-up hypothesis

## Draft prompt
> Create a LinkedIn post for [industrial audience] about [specific problem]. Funnel stage: [stage]. Purpose: [goal]. Evidence and approved facts: [facts/sources]. Format: [format]. Use a credible practitioner tone, clear paragraphs, one central idea, and a relevant CTA. Do not invent statistics, customer results, product capabilities, or urgency. Return a draft, suggested visual, claim check, and success metric.

## Analytics
Track available impressions/reach, relevant engagement, clicks and CTR, follower growth, page/profile visits, qualified comments and DMs, enquiries, meetings, landing-page conversion, and lead quality. Compare like with like over consistent windows. Account for small samples and distribution differences. Do not claim a post caused a lead without a credible attribution method.

## Guardrails
Never fabricate proof, quotes, statistics, or customer names. Avoid generic AI hype and making every post a product pitch. Use specific industrial language and keep safety/performance claims within approved evidence.
